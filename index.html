FAQ

Common Questions

What is InfoSense?

InfoSense is an AI-assisted information and fact-checking app for Android. You paste a claim, statement or piece of news, and InfoSense returns an easy-to-understand result — such as TRUE, FALSE, MISLEADING or UNVERIFIED — with an explanation and a confidence score.

Does InfoSense support Sinhala & English?

Yes. InfoSense is designed to provide useful analysis for Sinhala-speaking users, and English content is supported as well.

Is InfoSense always accurate?

No. InfoSense uses AI-assisted analysis, and AI can make mistakes. Results should be combined with your own judgment and reliable sources — especially for important or sensitive decisions.

Is InfoSense free?

InfoSense offers free daily checks with a usage limit, and a premium upgrade for unlimited daily checks and an ad-free experience.
