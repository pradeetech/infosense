Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about InfoSense.

What is InfoSense?
InfoSense is an AI-assisted information and fact-checking app for Android. You paste a claim, statement or piece of news, and InfoSense returns an easy-to-understand result — such as TRUE, FALSE, MISLEADING or UNVERIFIED — with an explanation and an estimated confidence score.
How does InfoSense verify information?
When you submit a claim, it is processed by an AI verification system (using services such as Google Gemini) which analyzes the content and generates a structured result with a verdict, confidence score, summary and key evidence points. Because this is AI-assisted analysis, results can contain errors — see our Disclaimer.
Does InfoSense support Sinhala?
Yes. InfoSense is designed to provide useful analysis for Sinhala-speaking users, and you can submit claims directly in Sinhala.
Does InfoSense support English?
Yes. English content is fully supported — you can submit and analyze claims in English as well.
Is InfoSense always accurate?
No. InfoSense uses AI-assisted analysis, and AI can make mistakes. Results may be incomplete, outdated or incorrect. Always use your own judgment and reliable sources — especially for important or sensitive decisions. Read more in our Disclaimer.
Can I check social-media posts?
Yes — copy the text of the post and paste it into the app for analysis. Please only submit content you have the right to share, and avoid including other people's private information.
Can I check news articles?
Yes. You can paste the article text directly, or use the Analyze a Link feature to submit an article link — InfoSense will analyze it and show signals such as source credibility, bias detection and misinformation risk.
Does InfoSense store my searches?
Submitted content may be processed by our backend systems and third-party AI services to provide the verification functionality. Usage information such as the number of verification requests and daily usage count may also be processed to enforce limits and improve the app. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
Is InfoSense free?
Yes. InfoSense offers free daily checks with a usage limit. You can start using the app for free and upgrade anytime if you need more.
Is there a premium version?
Yes. InfoSense Premium unlocks unlimited daily checks, an ad-free experience and priority access to the AI engine. You can upgrade from within the app.
How can I download the app?
Use the Download section on this website — it automatically fetches the latest release and gives you the APK file. You can also visit the GitHub Releases page directly.
Where can I report a problem?
You can email us at siriyalathaaththanayaka22@gmail.com or open an issue on our GitHub Issues page. See the Contact page for more options.
