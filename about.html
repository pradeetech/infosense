About

About InfoSense

Verify. Analyze. Trust.

In a world where information spreads faster than ever, knowing what to trust can be difficult. InfoSense was created to make information checking simpler and more accessible.

The Problem

Every day, millions of claims, screenshots, forwards and headlines fill our feeds. Some are true. Some are exaggerated. Some are completely false. And once a piece of misinformation is shared, it is very hard to take back.

Most people don't have the time or tools to verify every claim they see — especially when information is in Sinhala, where reliable fact-checking resources have traditionally been limited.

Our Approach

InfoSense brings AI-assisted analysis to your pocket. Instead of accepting or ignoring a suspicious claim, you can check it in seconds:

  • Paste it — submit a claim, statement, article text or link
  • Analyze it — AI processes the content and evaluates it
  • Understand it — get a clear verdict, a confidence score, a summary and key evidence points

Results are presented in simple categories — TRUE FALSE MISLEADING UNVERIFIED — so anyone can understand them, without needing special knowledge.

Who Is It For?

StudentsCheck facts for studies & projects
Social-media usersVerify before forwarding
News readersUnderstand headlines critically
EveryoneMake informed sharing decisions

Why Verify Before Sharing?

Sharing information is a responsibility. When you share something unverified, it can reach hundreds of people within minutes — and misinformation can cause real harm: panic, distrust, damaged reputations and bad decisions.

Think Before You Share. Verify With InfoSense. A few seconds of checking can make a real difference.

Honest note: InfoSense is an AI-assisted tool — not an absolute authority. Results can contain errors, and information can change over time. Always combine InfoSense results with your own judgment and reliable sources. Read our full Disclaimer.
