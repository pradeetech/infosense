InfoSense (“InfoSense,” “we,” “our,” or “us”), developed by PradeeTech, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when using the InfoSense mobile application, website, and related services. By using InfoSense, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1 About InfoSense

InfoSense is an AI-assisted information and fact-checking platform designed to help users analyze news, claims, statements, and other submitted content.

InfoSense may provide classifications such as:

TRUE FALSE MISLEADING UNVERIFIED

These results are generated with the assistance of artificial intelligence and should not be considered an absolute guarantee of factual accuracy.

Users should consult reliable and authoritative sources when making important decisions.

2 Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, InfoSense may collect or process the following categories of information.

2.1 — Account Information

If you create an account, you may sign in using Google Authentication or Email/Password. Depending on the method used, we may receive or store information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture URL
  • Firebase user identifier (UID)
  • Username chosen by you (if you create an email/password account)
  • Password (stored only as a secure hash by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your actual password)

This information is used to create and manage your InfoSense account. We do not request or intentionally collect your Google password.

2.2 — User-Submitted Content

When you submit text, news claims, statements, or other information for analysis, the submitted content may be processed by our backend systems and third-party AI services used to provide the requested verification functionality.

Depending on the application's implemented features, submitted content may include:

  • News claims
  • Social-media statements
  • Questions
  • Text copied from articles
  • Other information voluntarily entered by the user

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information, passwords, payment information, or confidential information into the fact-checking input.

2.3 — Usage Information

We may collect information related to your use of InfoSense, including:

  • Number of verification requests
  • Daily usage count
  • Account tier
  • Premium status
  • Feature usage
  • Authentication events
  • Error and diagnostic information

Usage information may be used to enforce account limits, maintain service security, and improve the application.

2.4 — Device and Technical Information

When you use the mobile application, certain technical information may be automatically processed by the application or third-party services, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Operating system version
  • Application version
  • Crash information
  • Basic diagnostic information
  • Network-related technical information

We use this information primarily for security, troubleshooting, analytics, and improving application stability.

2.5 — Website Information

When you visit the InfoSense website, the website may process limited technical information required for normal operation. The website may also use browser storage technologies such as local storage for functionality such as:

  • Theme preferences
  • User interface preferences
  • Temporary application state

If analytics or other tracking technologies are enabled on the website in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

3 How We Use Information

We may use information for the following purposes:

Account Management

  • Creating and maintaining user accounts
  • Authentication
  • Managing user sessions
  • Providing account-related functionality

AI-Powered Verification

  • Processing submitted claims
  • Generating AI-assisted analysis
  • Producing verification results
  • Providing explanations and supporting information where available

Usage Limits

  • Managing free-tier usage limits
  • Managing premium features
  • Preventing abuse of the service
  • Maintaining service availability

Security

  • Detecting suspicious activity
  • Preventing unauthorized access
  • Preventing abuse and automated attacks
  • Protecting the application and its users

Application Improvement

  • Understanding application performance
  • Diagnosing crashes and technical problems
  • Improving features and user experience
  • Monitoring service reliability

Advertising

If advertisements are enabled within InfoSense, advertising providers such as Google AdMob may process certain device or advertising-related information in accordance with their own policies and applicable settings.

4 Artificial Intelligence Processing

InfoSense uses artificial intelligence technology to assist with information analysis and fact-checking.

Depending on the implementation of the service, submitted content may be transmitted securely to an AI service provider such as Google Gemini for processing.

AI-generated results may contain errors, incomplete information, outdated information, or incorrect interpretations. Therefore:

InfoSense does not guarantee that every AI-generated verification result is completely accurate, current, or error-free.

Users should independently verify important information using reliable primary or authoritative sources. InfoSense should not be used as the sole basis for medical, legal, financial, emergency, or other high-impact decisions.

5 Third-Party Services

InfoSense may use third-party services to provide authentication, database, analytics, advertising, AI processing, crash reporting, hosting, or application update functionality. Depending on the features enabled in the application, these services may include:

Firebase

Firebase may be used for services such as:

  • Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Analytics
  • Crash reporting
  • Other application infrastructure

Google's Firebase privacy information is available through Google's official privacy resources.

Google Sign-In

If Google Sign-In is enabled, authentication is handled using Google's authentication infrastructure.

Google Gemini

Google Gemini may be used to process submitted content and generate AI-assisted analysis.

Google AdMob

If advertisements are enabled, Google AdMob may be used to display advertisements. Advertising providers may process information such as advertising identifiers or device-related information according to their policies and applicable privacy choices.

GitHub

The InfoSense website or application may access GitHub release information to identify and provide access to the latest publicly available application release.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Users should review the applicable policies of those providers.

6 Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information for monetary gain.

Information may nevertheless be processed by trusted service providers when necessary to operate InfoSense. Examples may include:

  • Authentication providers
  • Cloud database providers
  • AI service providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Crash-reporting providers
  • Advertising providers
  • Hosting or infrastructure providers

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law
  • Respond to lawful requests
  • Protect the rights and safety of users
  • Detect or prevent fraud or abuse
  • Protect the security of our services

7 Data Storage

Account-related information may be stored using cloud infrastructure such as Firebase/Google Cloud services.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8 Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including:

  • Providing the requested service
  • Maintaining an active account
  • Enforcing usage limits
  • Maintaining security
  • Meeting legitimate operational requirements
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it is processed.

10 Children's Privacy

InfoSense is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to InfoSense, please contact us so that appropriate steps can be taken.

Where local law establishes a different minimum age for online services or consent, the applicable legal requirement may apply.

11 Advertising and Personalization

If advertisements are displayed through Google AdMob or another advertising provider, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized depending on the provider's configuration, user settings, consent requirements, and applicable law.

Users may be able to control certain advertising or privacy settings through their device or the relevant advertising provider.

InfoSense does not directly control all information processed by third-party advertising networks.

12 Cookies and Local Storage

The InfoSense website may use browser storage technologies, including local storage, to remember certain preferences and support website functionality. These may include:

  • Theme preference
  • Interface preferences
  • Temporary settings

If cookies, analytics, or additional tracking technologies are introduced, the relevant information will be reflected in an updated version of this Privacy Policy where required.

13 Security

We use reasonable security measures designed to protect information handled by InfoSense. These may include:

  • HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Security Rules
  • Server-side authorization
  • Access controls
  • Secure API handling
  • Rate limiting and abuse prevention where implemented

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14 International Data Processing

Some third-party service providers used by InfoSense may process information on servers located outside your country of residence.

By using InfoSense, you acknowledge that information may be processed internationally where necessary to provide the service, subject to applicable laws and the privacy practices of the relevant service providers.

15 Your Responsibilities

When using InfoSense, you should avoid submitting:

  • Passwords
  • Payment-card information
  • Government identification numbers
  • Highly sensitive personal information
  • Confidential business information
  • Private information belonging to another person

InfoSense is designed to analyze information, not to act as a secure storage system for confidential information.

16 AI Verification Disclaimer

InfoSense provides AI-assisted information analysis, not guaranteed truth.

A result such as TRUE does not mean that InfoSense guarantees the claim is correct in every context.

Likewise, FALSE, MISLEADING, or UNVERIFIED does not constitute a legal, professional, or authoritative determination.

Information can change over time, particularly in relation to breaking news and developing events. Users should review original sources and authoritative information before relying on a result.

We may update this Privacy Policy when:

  • New features are introduced
  • Third-party services change
  • Data-processing practices change
  • Security practices change
  • Legal or regulatory requirements change

When changes are made, the Last Updated date at the top of this document will be updated. For significant changes, additional notice may be provided through the application or website where appropriate.
