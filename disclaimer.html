AI-Assisted Analysis

InfoSense uses AI-assisted analysis to evaluate submitted claims and information. Artificial intelligence has inherent limitations.

AI can make mistakes. Results may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect — and information can change over time, especially in breaking news and developing events.

1 What Results Mean

InfoSense may present results such as:

TRUE FALSE MISLEADING UNVERIFIED

These results are not absolute guarantees. A "TRUE" result does not mean a claim is correct in every context. A "FALSE", "MISLEADING" or "UNVERIFIED" result is not a legal, professional, or authoritative determination.

Confidence scores are estimates that show how strongly the system supports its assessment — they are not measurements of truth.

2 Not Professional Advice

InfoSense is not a substitute for professional legal, medical, financial, emergency, or other expert advice.

For important decisions — health, money, safety, legal matters — always consult qualified professionals and authoritative sources.

3 Your Responsibility

You are responsible for how you use the information and results provided by InfoSense.

  • Consult reliable and authoritative sources for important matters
  • Use your own judgment before sharing information
  • Do not rely on InfoSense as the sole basis for any decision
  • Review original sources whenever possible
